The shop

A straight answer before a single bolt turns.

Most people arrive at a repair shop already braced for the part they did not expect. We would rather take that out of the equation. You get the diagnosis in plain language, the price before the work starts, and a call if anything changes along the way.

We work on daily drivers and the cars people have kept for a long time. Brakes, suspension, engine and transmission, electrical faults, and the noises that only happen on the way to work. If it is not worth fixing, we will tell you that too.

How a repair goes.

Four steps, and you hear from us at each one.

  1. You book or call

    Tell us the car and what it is doing. The booking form takes about a minute and asks the questions we would ask on the phone anyway.

  2. We look at it

    Diagnosis first. We find the actual cause rather than replacing parts until the symptom goes away.

  3. You get the number

    Parts, labour, and how long it will take, before we begin. Nothing goes on the bill that you did not approve.

  4. You pick it up

    We walk you through what was done and what to keep an eye on next. No upsell attached.

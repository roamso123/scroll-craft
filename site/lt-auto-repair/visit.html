Before you drop off.

  1. Write down when it happens

    Cold start, highway speed, turning left, braking hard. When a fault happens is often more useful than what it sounds like.

  2. Leave a number we can reach

    We call before any work is authorised. If we cannot reach you, the car waits.

  3. Clear the back seat and trunk

    Only if the job touches the interior or the spare well. The form will say if it does.

  4. Bring anything on paper

    Recent receipts, a previous shop's estimate, or the report that came with a used car. It saves diagnostic time.

Services

Four kinds of glass, one standard.

Tell us what the glass is and what you want from it. We will tell you the film, the shade and the time it takes.

01

Automotive tinting

Cars, SUVs, trucks and vans. Film is cut to your glass, heat-shrunk to the curve of the rear window and tucked behind the seals rather than trimmed against them. Most vehicles are done the same day.

Full vehicle

Every window the law allows, matched shade front to back.

Rear glass only

Back window and rear doors, the legal envelope on most cars.

Sunstrip and brow

A band across the top of the windshield to cut low-sun glare.

Ceramic film

Rejects heat without going darker, and keeps signal through the glass.

02

Home and office glass

A south-facing room that cooks from two in the afternoon is a film problem, not an air conditioning problem. We measure on site and fit flat glass film to the pane.

Heat and glare

Cuts the afternoon load on living rooms, kitchens and screens.

Privacy film

Daytime privacy for ground floors without losing the view out.

Frosted and decorative

Bathrooms, partitions and glass doors, cut to a clean line.

UV protection

Slows the fading of floors, furniture and artwork near the window.

Storefront and fleet

Shopfront glazing

Large panes, consistent shade across a whole frontage.

Fleet vehicles

Vans and work trucks, uniform across the fleet.

Security film

04

Removal and repair

Old film goes purple, bubbles at the top of the door glass and peels at the corners. We strip it, clean the adhesive off the defroster lines without lifting them, and refit.

Full strip

Film and adhesive removed from every treated pane.

Bubble and peel fixes

A single failed window redone rather than the whole car.

Defroster safe

Rear glass worked carefully so the heating lines survive the strip.

Refit after removal

Fresh film laid the same visit where time allows.

Before you come

A few things worth knowing.

The law, plainly

New Jersey permits tint on rear side and back windows. Front side windows and the windshield stay clear unless you hold a medical exemption.

Leave the windows up

Fresh film needs a few days to cure. We will tell you how long for your car and the weather.

Send the details ahead

The booking survey takes under a minute and saves the back and forth at the counter.

Old film is fine

Bring it in bubbled or purple. Stripping and refitting is routine work here.

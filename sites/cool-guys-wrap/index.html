Seven finishes.

The finish changes how the car reads far more than the colour does. Apply one and watch the car above.

What we wrap.

Full wrap
Every painted panel, with the film tucked behind the edges and post-heated so it stays put.
Partial wrap
Roof, bonnet, boot lid, or a single quarter. The usual first job.
Chrome delete
Window trim, badges and grille surrounds taken to gloss or satin black.
Accents
Mirrors, splitter, diffuser, wing, interior trim pieces.
Removal
Old film off, adhesive cleaned down, paint checked before anything new goes on.

Every job is quoted on the car. Panel count, paint condition and the film you choose all move the number, so we price it once we have seen it.

Four steps.

From the first look at the car to the drive home.

Book a look

Bring the car in or send photos. We check the paint, the panel gaps and any film already on it.

Quote on the car

We measure the panels and price the job in front of you. Colour and finish get locked here.

Strip and wrap

Handles, badges and trim come off. The surface is cleaned down and each panel is wrapped in one piece.

Walk and hand over

We go around the car with you, then show you how to wash it so the edges stay down.

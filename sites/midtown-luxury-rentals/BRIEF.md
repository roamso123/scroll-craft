## The eight topics

**1. Vibe, three to five words.** Lit studio floor. Black room, one light, one car.
References, none from the web: a Mercedes press-kit contact sheet; the hour a
showroom is shut and only the ceiling strip is on; a museum object card.

**2. The scroll journey.** Authored from the user's sequence ("demonstrate these
stills" first, "call to actions to rent now" throughout, booking survey at the click):

1. One car, lit, already labelled. No title screen in front of it.
2. The collection travelling sideways past the reader.
3. Inside. The cabin, held still, after the rush.
4. The four ways to use the company: self-drive, chauffeur, Sprinter, private jet.
5. Where it comes from and where it goes. Real address, real states.
6. The inquiry plate. The survey opens.

**3. The energy curve.** Calm at the open (one object, no noise), loudest across the
collection, quiet in the cabin, level through services and reach, quiet and
resolved at the plate.

**4. The feeling curve, and the peak.**

| Act | Feeling | What on screen causes it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Arrival | Stillness, then recognition | A Maybach at full bleed on a black floor, its build sheet already filled in. No headline stacked over it |
| 2 The collection | Appetite | Cars travelling right to left while the fixed build sheet re-reads itself, horsepower sweeping between the figures of one car and the next |
| 3 Inside | Intimacy | One cabin, one frame, a wipe. Nothing moves laterally |
| 4 Four ways | Clarity | Four labelled objects, same schema, no persuasion |
| 5 Reach | Reassurance | A real street address and four real state codes counting in |
| 6 The plate | Decision | An inquiry card typeset exactly like the object labels, with the survey behind it |

Adjacent acts differ: stillness, appetite, intimacy, clarity, reassurance, decision.

**The peak:** act 2, the collection. It holds the largest span on the page by a
clear margin, and act 1 before it is the quietest thing on the site.

> The sentence a visitor would say to a friend:
> "The spec panel at the bottom kept re-reading itself as the cars went past. The
> horsepower literally swept from one car's number to the next one's."

**5. One thing no other site does.** The build sheet is not a caption and it is not a
summary. It is the booking control. Whatever car currently owns the frame is the car
the button is loaded with, and the figures interpolate rather than switch, so the
readout is continuous with the scroll rather than stepped by it.

**6. Distance from premium-minimal.** Premium-minimal, earned rather than defaulted:
the brand is luxury vehicle hire and the supplied stills are all a single studio
shoot on a black floor. The red is the one accent and it is used as signal, per the
user's instruction, not as decoration.

**7. One unbroken world, or distinct scenes?** Distinct objects. This is a
collection, not a journey. Each vehicle lands whole and leaves whole.

**8. Assets already held.** The brand logo, and a single-source studio still set:
Maybach S 580 (front, rear, cabin) and BMW M5 (rear) on disk; M4 Competition
Convertible, M5 front, G 63, Escalade, 911 Carrera S, C 63, and four cabin frames
re-dropping into named slots.

## Authored silence

Act 3 (the cabin) is deliberately near-motionless. One wipe, one line of label copy,
no lateral travel, no counters. It exists to make act 2 land, and a verification pass
should read it as intended quiet, not as dead scroll.

---

## Hard content rules for this build

- **No invented numbers.** No daily rates, no fake review counts, no made-up fleet
  statistics. Vehicle figures are manufacturer specification and are labelled as such
  on the page. The only company figures used are ones the business publishes: the
  showroom address, the two phone numbers, and the four states it delivers to.
- **Object labels carry fact, not pitch.** Same schema on every vehicle, every service.
- **Rate on request** is the price line, everywhere, without exception.

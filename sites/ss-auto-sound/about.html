The shop

A small shop on
East Tremont Avenue.

SS Auto Sound is a Bronx car accessories and installation shop. Vehicles pull up out front, businesses call us in, and homes get the same crew.

We work on what we can finish properly. That means one job at a time on the bench, a quote before the panels come off, and a car handed back with the interior sitting the way the factory left it.

The commercial and residential side grew out of the same habit. A customer who liked how their truck turned out asked about their storefront, then their living room. The tools change, the standard does not: hide the wire, kill the rattle, tune it until nobody notices the equipment.

No showroom theatrics and no upsell script. Tell us the budget and we will tell you honestly what it buys.

How it works

Four steps, no mystery.

01

Call or walk in

Describe the vehicle or the space. Most quotes take one phone call; complicated jobs we come look at.

02

We spec it to your budget

Gear chosen for the room or the cabin, not the invoice. You approve the number and the date before anything starts.

03

Install day

Most automotive work is same day. Commercial and residential runs are scheduled around your hours so you are not shut down.

04

Tune, walk through, warranty

We set it with you there, show you how to run it, and stand behind the install afterward.

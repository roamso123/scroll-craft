How it got here

From court to kitchen

Eddie Lampkin Jr. came out of Houston, played at TCU, then Colorado, then signed with the Orange. He met Antoine in Colorado. When Eddie came to Syracuse, Antoine and his family came too, and Antoine runs the kitchen with his wife and daughter.

The restaurant opened on West Water Street with both of their names on it and a mural of the whole story on the wall.

The walls are the story

Hand-painted comic panels, the jerseys in frames, Chicken Express, drive thru, Eddie and I meal, OK cool. Every booth sits under a piece of how two friends ended up cooking in Syracuse.
